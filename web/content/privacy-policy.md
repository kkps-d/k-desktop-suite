# Privacy Policy for KD Notepad and KD Paint

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains how **KD Notepad** and **KD Paint**, developed by
**kkps-d**, handle your information. For support or privacy questions, contact
[kkps.developer@gmail.com](mailto:kkps.developer@gmail.com).

## Information handled by the apps

KD Notepad and KD Paint process your documents and images on your device. The
apps do not send document contents, images, usage information, or device
identifiers to the developer. They have no user accounts, advertising,
analytics, or automatic crash reporting.

To provide editing and recovery features, the apps handle:

- Text or images you create, open, paste, or provide through drag and drop.
- File names and locations for opening, saving, and showing recent files.
- Local recovery copies of your work and information needed to restore your
  editing session.
- Preferences such as appearance, language, app icon, fonts, and drawing settings.

These features work without giving the apps broad access to your device's
storage. Android controls access to files you choose, and the apps may retain
permission to reopen or save those files later.

## Files, clipboard, and sharing

You choose where to open and save files through Android's document picker. If
you select a cloud storage provider, that provider may transfer and store your
files under its own privacy policy. The apps do not operate their own cloud
storage or synchronization service.

Copying content places it on Android's clipboard. Pasting reads the clipboard
content you choose to use. KD Paint also lets you share an exported image with
an app or recipient you select through Android's share sheet. The receiving app,
storage provider, or service handles that content under its own policies.

We do not sell your information or send your documents to advertisers.

## Local storage, retention, and deletion

Preferences, recent-file information, and recovery drafts are stored in the
app's private storage. Recovery copies can remain so the app can restore your
work after you close it or restart your device. They are separate from files
you explicitly save.

KD Paint also stores local copies used for saving, sharing, and image clipboard
operations. When it creates another export, it attempts to remove these copies
if they are more than seven days old. Copies may remain longer if no further
export occurs.

You can remove an app's private data, including its drafts, preferences, and
local export copies, by clearing its storage in Android Settings or uninstalling
it. Save any drafts you want to keep first. Files saved to a location you chose,
or copies already received by another app or service, must be deleted separately
from that location or service. Removing a recent-file entry in KD Paint does
not delete the original file.

## Security

The apps use Android's private app storage for internal data and Android-granted
permissions for access to selected files. Sharing an image grants the receiving
app access to the exported copy. Protection of saved files, clipboard contents,
and shared copies also depends on your device settings and the apps or storage
services you choose.

## Contacting support

If you email [kkps.developer@gmail.com](mailto:kkps.developer@gmail.com), we receive
your email address and any information or attachments you choose to send. We
use that information to respond to your inquiry and provide support. Our email
provider processes messages to deliver and store them.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to address your inquiry and related
follow-up. You can request its deletion at the same address, subject to any
applicable legal retention requirements. The apps do not automatically attach
your documents or diagnostic information to support messages.

## Changes to this policy

We will publish updates on this page and revise the date above when our data
handling practices change.

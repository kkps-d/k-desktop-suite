Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: high-fidelity Android app UI design board for the KD Paint project.
Primary request: create a polished, implementation-oriented UI mockup for an offline raster drawing app built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3 Expressive. Show an expanded landscape editor and its compact portrait phone adaptation together in one landscape image. This is a credible application screenshot mockup with precise, legible UI, not concept art or a marketing landing page.

Composition: flat straight-on 2D presentation on a very light warm-gray design-board background, ample outer margins. Main expanded editor on the left, roughly 74% of available horizontal space; a separate portrait phone screen on the right, roughly 24%, with a small gutter and both artboards fully visible. Small restrained heading above the board: "KD Paint". Smaller subheading: "Adaptive Android editor". Each screen has a very thin rounded outline and minimal shadow. No device photography, no perspective, no giant decorative hero. Labels "Expanded" and "Compact" outside the app artboards. Render at a generous landscape resolution so small UI labels remain readable.

Design system: authentic contemporary Material 3 Expressive, light theme, restrained forest/sage green accents consistent with a green fallback Android palette, off-white neutral surfaces, clear charcoal typography in Roboto-like Android sans serif, softly rounded tonal controls and a few pill-shaped action buttons, crisp 1.5-2px outline icons with consistent visual weight, small visual footprints but ample touch space. Flat quiet workspace, thin dividers, minimal elevation; no glass, gradients, loud saturated green panels, or inflated bubbly chrome. Visible selected controls have a pale green fill and dark green icon. The drawing canvas is pure white, independent of theme.

Expanded editor exact layout:
1. One compact top app bar: leading small green paintbrush app icon and "KD Paint", then document title "Garden sketch.png" with a small unsaved dot. Trailing Undo icon, Redo icon with subdued disabled state, a green tonal "Save" button, and vertical three-dot overflow. No extra File/Home/View menus or tabs.
2. Under the app bar, leading tool strip about 56dp wide, center large canvas viewport, trailing properties pane about 300dp wide. Separate strips and pane by subtle dividers. Leading strip holds clear icons for Pencil, Brush, Fill, Eraser, Text (T), Eyedropper, rectangular Select, Shape, Zoom, and Pan. Brush is selected in a green rounded rectangle, with a tiny "Brush" label if space permits. This strip is drawing tools, not bottom navigation destinations.
3. Center viewport has a quiet pale gray-green workspace. Large white 4:3 canvas, anchored near the upper leading area with a subtle border and modest surrounding workspace. Show a charming but simple editable raster drawing: a hand-painted terracotta pot with three leafy green stems, a small muted golden sun, a few gentle pencil marks and understated brush texture, lots of white negative space. Artwork should feel produced by drawing tools and should not compete with the UI. No text in the artwork. No checkerboard transparency, no selection overlay, no layer handles. A small brush cursor ring near a leaf.
4. Trailing properties pane headed "Brush", with small collapse chevron. First section labeled "Style", a "Solid" dropdown and a tidy 3-by-3 gallery of nine distinct miniature stroke swatches; first is selected. Next "Width" with value "3 px", Material slider, and preset chips "1", "3", "5", "8" with 3 selected. Next "Colors": clearly named "Foreground" green swatch and "Background" white swatch, a swap icon between them, then a compact palette of twenty colors and ten small custom-color slots, followed by "Edit colors". Keep the text and controls comfortably spaced. No opacity control and no fill tolerance.
5. Thin bottom information strip: left "800 × 600 px", right outlined "Fit" chip, zoom out icon, "78%", zoom in icon. Keep this quiet and the canvas dominant.

Compact portrait phone adaptation:
Android status bar at the top with time "9:41", simple signal/battery icons; bottom Android gesture handle. One app bar with title "Garden sketch.png", small unsaved dot, Undo, Save icon, and overflow. At narrower width, no extra brand wordmark crowding the title. Same white 4:3 drawing canvas in a quiet viewport, scaled to fit, with the same terracotta pot and green plant artwork. Under the viewport, small image info "800 × 600 px" and a "Fit" zoom control. At bottom above gesture inset, compact tools row with Brush selected, Eraser, Text, Select, Shape, and More tools icon. A small contextual row directly above it shows current brush "Solid", green foreground/white background swatches, and labeled "Options" button. This is not navigation. Do not squeeze the expanded properties pane onto the phone. Keep all controls within the screen and do not obscure the drawing.

Constraints: use the exact provided short UI labels and spell "KD Paint" correctly. Text must be clean and legible. Keep realistic alignment, breathing room, consistent corner radii and contrast, accessible touch target spacing. Android visual language only. No Microsoft/Windows branding, no ribbon, no classic desktop menu bar, no faux minimize/maximize buttons, no iOS controls, no tabs, no floating action button covering artwork, no layers, no AI, no accounts, no advertising, no extra charts or annotations inside the app. Show only the two specified artboards and restrained board headings.
